## Overview

EcoWave answers a simple question: why should technological progress conflict with protecting the planet?

It is a sustainable smartwatch built with recycled and carefully selected materials, powered in part by solar charging. The brand targets people aged 25–45 across Europe who care about technology **and** the impact of what they buy—without forcing a trade-off between design and values.

> *“Technology can measure time. We want time to measure for something more.”*

### Values
- **Innovation** — smarter, more sustainable solutions  
- **Sustainability** — recycled materials and solar energy  
- **Lifestyle** — fits everyday modern living  
- **Responsibility** — every purchase can have a more positive impact
